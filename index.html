Shopping Information

Please read our shopping guidelines before ordering.

Ordering Policy

Orders are submitted through WhatsApp and are confirmed only after the business reviews and acknowledges them.

Payment Policy

Payment methods and instructions will be confirmed directly with the business before payment.

Delivery Policy

Delivery availability, charges and estimated delivery times will be agreed before an order is completed.

Returns & Exchanges

Confirm size, colour and product details before ordering. Contact us directly about any order issue.

Custom Stitching

Custom designs require agreement on measurements, materials, design details, price and completion time.

Privacy

Your contact and delivery information is used to communicate about your order.

Confirm the final store policies with the business owner before launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy something?

Add products to your bag, proceed to checkout and send your order through WhatsApp.

Can I order custom-made outfits?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp to discuss your design, measurements, pricing and availability.

How do I make payment?

Payment arrangements will be confirmed directly with the business after your order is reviewed.

Do you deliver?

Contact us to confirm delivery availability, charges and estimated delivery time.

Can I ask about product sizes?

Yes. Send us a WhatsApp message before ordering to confirm available sizes and fit information.
